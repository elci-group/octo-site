About octo-site

A proof that a grow site can live on a public git host and still run entirely on your machine.

This site exists to exercise the grow:// browser flow: a Chrome extension that turns grow://github.com/<owner>/<repo> URLs into locally-served pages, and navigation within grow sites through ordinary links and grow:// links alike.

You arrived here through a grow:// link — the extension intercepted the click, the daemon resolved the repo to its running localhost server, and the browser navigated to it. The stylesheet on this page came from the same verified commit as the HTML.
